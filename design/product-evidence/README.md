# AI7Lab product evidence selection

Prepared: 13 October 2026

This pack records the product visuals selected for the next design pass. It contains only public Vendoreye material and public-safe views from the local RoleField project. Customer records, credentials, internal identifiers, and unapproved performance figures are excluded.

## Vendoreye

Primary visual:

- **Procurement Command Center** — official platform screenshot published by Vendoreye with fictional sample data.
- Source page: `https://www.vendoreye.ae/`
- Original asset: `https://www.vendoreye.ae/static/marketing-v2/previews/dashboard-1600.webp`
- Local retained copy: `vendor-eye-procurement-command-center.webp`

Supporting experience references:

1. **Connected procurement journey** — Discover, Qualify, Source, Deliver, Track commercials, Improve.
2. **Vik supplier-onboarding workflow** — Read, Check, Clarify, Advance, followed by buyer review.
3. **Vendor network proof** — the public site currently reports 134,231 listed vendors; the AI7Lab prototype retains the safer approved planning expression `134,000+` until claim governance is closed.

Recommended use:

- Use the Command Center screenshot as the principal Vendoreye product visual.
- Rebuild the six-stage journey as an interactive guided explanation rather than a static screenshot.
- Keep the fictional-sample-data disclosure adjacent to the product visual.

## RoleField

Source project:

- `/Users/zainabkhan/Documents/ChatGPT/Rolefield`
- Local review URL: `http://127.0.0.1:3000/`

Selected public-safe views:

1. **RoleField Control Room** (`#platform`) — live-operations state, work in progress, measured impact, multilingual interaction rows, follow-up, CRM update, and human-handover states.
2. **Complete customer workflow** (`#agents`, beneath the agent cards) — Find the next customer, Call and understand, Schedule, Follow up, Complete the record.
3. **Multilingual agent configuration** (`#languages`) — language, voice, role, and configured-agent result.
4. **Configurable business outcomes** (`#use-cases`) — problem, RoleField action, and outcome across support, revenue, operations, finance, hospitality, property, banking, and logistics.

Not selected for the public product story:

- The final RoleField platform-management illustration contains interface sample data that is not needed for the public product story.
- Authenticated OLV mailbox and administration screens are not representative of the core RoleField customer-operations proposition and should not lead the AI7Lab product story.

Recommended use:

- Lead with the Control Room as the real product-operating view.
- Use the five-stage workflow as the interactive explanation of how RoleField moves beyond conversation.
- Use the language configurator as supporting proof of GCC and multilingual product depth.
- Keep Seer as the physical-service surface within the wider RoleField operating platform.

## Public-use safeguards

- Do not show live customer data or credentials.
- Do not imply illustrative interfaces are live customer deployments.
- Preserve product-source disclosures.
- Keep each operating metric grouped with the product it describes.
