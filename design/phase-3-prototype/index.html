RoleField · Seer

An AI front desk that can recognise context.

Seer is RoleField's kiosk AI experience for approved physical environments. It can recognise enrolled visitors, retrieve relevant profile context, start a device-trained conversation, retain interaction history, and capture supported documents.

  • Approved-person recognition and contextual profiles
  • Device-specific conversational behaviour
  • Returning-visitor and conversation continuity
  • Business-card, Emirates ID and passport capture modes
  • Animated avatar interfaces for kiosk experiences
