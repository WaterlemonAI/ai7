# AI7Lab website rehaul — Phase 2 messaging and information architecture

Prepared: 13 October 2026  
Status: Design-ready strategic specification  
Inputs: Phase 1 audit, current site content, approved portfolio hierarchy, and supplied business metrics

## 1. Strategic direction

AI7Lab should be presented first as a product-building company, second as an enterprise AI partner, and third as a source of useful research and technical tools.

The website must make four ideas clear within the first screen and the following proof section:

1. AI7Lab builds products powered by agentic AI.
2. Its products already operate at meaningful scale.
3. AI7Lab also works directly with organisations on valuable operational problems.
4. Its technology is designed to evolve as models, infrastructure, and interfaces change.

Sovereignty, governance, private deployment, infrastructure, and model control remain credible supporting capabilities. They should appear where relevant to enterprise trust, not as the opening definition of the company.

## 2. Approved portfolio hierarchy

### Principal products

1. **Vendoreye** — supplier and opportunity intelligence.
2. **RoleField** — an AI operating platform for customer support, calling, call-centre operations, and physical-service kiosks through Seer.

These receive the strongest navigation, homepage prominence, proof, and conversion paths.

### Tools built by AI7Lab

1. **Taed** — visual and document intelligence that turns documents, images, video, and audio into structured, schema-controlled outputs through versioned and monitored APIs.

Taed demonstrates how AI7Lab turns research into reusable production capability. It is not presented as a third peer product, but it receives a substantial showcase and retains its existing search content.

### Enterprise work

AI7Lab works with organisations to identify, validate, build, integrate, and improve AI systems around important business problems. The offer is problem-led rather than a generic consulting catalogue.

## 4. Message hierarchy

### Message 1 — We build, not just advise

AI7Lab owns and operates products. Enterprise customers should see the team as product builders who understand the realities of deployment, integration, measurement, failure, and scale.

Proof:

- Vendoreye and RoleField are live products.
- Taed is a production-oriented tool created from AI7Lab research.
- Existing research covers production readiness, security, governance, evaluation, and operating economics.

### Message 2 — Our products operate at scale

Proof, subject to final claim definitions and dates:

- Vendoreye: more than 134,000 vendors.
- Vendoreye: more than 20 organisations using or trusting the platform for opportunity discovery.
- RoleField: more than 60 million calls.
- RoleField: approximately 10 million calls per month.
- RoleField: more than US$1 million in customer savings.
- Taed: published milestone of more than 1,000 API calls per day.

The design must make the source and scope of each metric legible. Vendoreye metrics must not visually appear to describe RoleField, and vice versa.

### Message 3 — We start with business value

AI7Lab looks for problems where intelligent systems can reduce manual work, remove operational friction, lower cost, increase consistency, or create a capability that was previously impractical.

The company should not promise automation for every process. The narrative should emphasize selecting the right problem and retaining human authority where decisions require it.

### Message 4 — Agentic AI means action, not another interface

Recommended plain-language definition:

> Agentic AI combines models, data, tools, and workflow logic so a system can understand context, take approved actions, handle exceptions, and complete useful work—not only generate an answer.

### Message 5 — Future-proof means replaceable and observable

Avoid claiming that any technology can literally be future-proof. Use the language “designed to evolve” and support it with concrete principles:

- Modular model and infrastructure choices.
- Replaceable components and stable interfaces.
- Versioned prompts, workflows, schemas, and policies.
- Continuous evaluation and monitoring.
- Controlled integrations with enterprise systems.
- Human approvals and escalation paths.
- Feedback loops that improve the product over time.

### Message 6 — The lab keeps looking for the next valuable problem

AI7Lab is not limited to its current products. The company continuously studies workflow problems, tests technical possibilities, and builds tools or products when there is a credible path to ecosystem value.

This idea belongs near the Taed showcase and company story, not as an unsupported promise that many new products are imminent.

## 5. Audience hierarchy

### Primary audience A — Enterprise decision-makers

Typical roles:

- CEOs and business-unit leaders.
- CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, and heads of AI.
- Operations, transformation, and shared-services leaders.
- Procurement and supplier-management leaders.
- Customer operations, contact-center, sales, and service leaders.

Their questions:

- Where can AI create a measurable result?
- Can this team move beyond a demonstration?
- How will it integrate with our systems and controls?
- What evidence exists that it can operate at scale?
- How do we start without committing to a large transformation programme?

Primary journey:

> Homepage → relevant product or Enterprise AI → proof and operating approach → discuss a problem

### Primary audience B — Product buyers

Vendoreye audience:

- Procurement teams.
- Supplier and vendor-management teams.
- Third-party-risk and compliance teams.
- Organizations looking for qualified opportunities or vendor discovery.

RoleField audience:

- Contact-center and customer-operations leaders.
- Sales and lead-qualification teams.
- Service, scheduling, collections, and high-volume calling operations.
- Teams requiring multilingual or Arabic-capable voice workflows.
- Organisations operating receptions, branches, events, or other approved physical environments that need contextual kiosk AI.

Primary journey:

> Search, campaign, or homepage → product detail → product evidence and use cases → visit product or request a conversation

### Secondary audience — Technology and ecosystem partners

Needs:

- Understand AI7Lab’s product portfolio and technical capabilities.
- Identify integration, infrastructure, distribution, or delivery fit.
- See credible areas for collaboration without dominating the primary narrative.

### Secondary audience — Talent and researchers

Needs:

- Understand the problems AI7Lab works on.
- See evidence of production scale and technical seriousness.
- Review open roles and the team’s operating principles.

```text
│   └── /insights/ai-in-x/[slug]
├── /vision                    Navigation label: Company
├── /careers
└── /contact
```

### Preserved supporting estate

```text
/announcements
/partners
/partners/builders
/partners/enterprises
/partners/technology-providers
/platform
/resources/*
/llms.txt
/llms-full.txt
/robots.txt
/sitemap.xml
```

The supporting estate remains indexable but does not need to appear in primary navigation. Consolidation or redirects should wait for analytics and backlink data.

### Why `/vision` is retained for Company

The site already owns and indexes `/vision`. Reusing it for the broader company story avoids introducing `/about` and immediately redirecting an established URL without evidence. The navigation can display “Company” while the canonical path remains `/vision`.

### Why Enterprise AI receives a new route

`/enterprise-ai` expresses the new offer more clearly than `/partners/enterprises`. The existing enterprise partner page remains live and can link to the new page. A later redirect can be considered after traffic and backlink review.

### Why Taed lives under Technology

`/technology/taed` signals that Taed is a substantial AI7Lab-built tool without presenting it as a third principal product. Product overview and relevant articles should link to this showcase.

1. Agentic systems and workflow automation.
2. Voice AI and customer operations.
3. Procurement and supplier intelligence.
4. Document and visual intelligence.
5. Enterprise AI strategy, economics, and readiness.

Recommended filters:

- Topic.
- Industry.
- Content type: research, practical guide, checklist, template, presentation.

Each article should end with one contextually relevant next step rather than the same generic CTA:

- Enterprise strategy article → Enterprise AI page.
- Checklist/resource → related download and enterprise discussion.

## 11. Content and claim rules

### Evidence hierarchy

Tier 1 — stable facts:

- Company identity and Dubai/DIFC location.
- Product and tool ownership.
- Documented capabilities already present in source.
- Dated historical announcements.

Tier 2 — approved metrics:

- Vendor counts, organisation counts, call volumes, savings, and API volumes.
- Display only with agreed definitions and a measurement date.

Tier 3 — illustrative material:

- Workflow diagrams, sample interactions, and hypothetical use cases.
- Label these as illustrative rather than customer evidence.

### Voice and tone

- Direct, precise, and commercially literate.
- Confident without claiming inevitability or universal automation.
- Technical enough to establish credibility, but led by business outcomes.
- Prefer evidence and concrete workflows over adjectives.
- Use British English consistently: organisations, prioritisation, programme.

### Language to prefer

- Agentic AI products.
- Systems that take approved action.
- Production workflows.
- Measurable operational outcomes.
- Designed to evolve.
- Human authority and escalation.
- Evidence-backed decisions.

### Language to avoid

- Revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, or limitless.
- Fully autonomous unless a specific workflow genuinely is.
- Future-proof as an absolute claim.
- “AI transformation” without naming the actual work or outcome.
- Unqualified savings, accuracy, scale, or customer-trust claims.

## 14. Assumptions carried into design

Until the business confirms otherwise, the Figma design should assume:

- Enterprise problem discussions are the primary site-level conversion.
- Vendoreye and RoleField receive equal portfolio prominence, with proof density adjusted to available evidence.
- Taed appears in the product ecosystem but is visually labelled “Tool built by AI7Lab.”
- Customer logos and named testimonials are unavailable.
- Metrics should remain grouped with the product they describe and use consistent wording across pages.
- Existing partnership programmes and job listings remain accessible but secondary.
- The existing Render deployment is production and remains completely untouched.
- The redesign will be implemented and validated as a separate AWS-hosted site before any production-domain decision.
- AWS staging will be kept out of search results to prevent duplicate indexing.
