# AI7Lab website rehaul — Phase 2 page briefs

These briefs are the content basis for low-fidelity Figma wireframes. They define sequence and substance, not visual composition.

Hosting constraint: the current Render deployment remains untouched. The approved redesign will be implemented and tested on a separate AWS environment after Figma sign-off.

## Homepage `/`

### Objective

Explain what AI7Lab is, demonstrate real scale, introduce the portfolio hierarchy, and move qualified visitors toward a product or business-problem conversation.

### Narrative sequence

1. **Hero**
   - “AI products built to do the work.”
   - Company descriptor and two CTAs.
   - Visual concept should express systems moving from context to action, not a decorative AI orb.

2. **Proof at operating scale**
   - Vendoreye metrics grouped together.
   - RoleField metrics grouped together.
   - Dates/definitions included in supporting text.

3. **Two principal products**
   - Vendoreye: supplier and opportunity intelligence.
   - RoleField: customer support, calling, call-centre operations, and Seer kiosk AI.
   - Each product has its own visual language, proof, and next step.

4. **From a business problem to a working system**
   - Explain the enterprise offer.
   - Suggested sequence: frame → prove → integrate → operate and improve.

5. **Built by the lab: Taed**
   - Demonstrate document/media input becoming schema-controlled output.
   - Link to the detailed Taed showcase and research.

6. **Designed to evolve**
   - Modular components, replaceable models, controlled integrations, evaluation, monitoring, and human escalation.

7. **What we are exploring**
   - Selected research and insight cards by topic.
   - Reinforce the continuing search for valuable problems.

8. **Closing conversion**
   - “Bring us the problem worth solving.”
   - Primary CTA: Discuss a problem.
   - Secondary routes to Vendoreye and RoleField.

### Avoid

- Recreating the current seven-discipline loop as the homepage backbone.
- Repeating the same product claims in multiple sections.
- Giving Taed equal card treatment with the two principal products.
- A wall of research cards before the business story is understood.

## Vendoreye `/products/vendoreye`

### Objective

Convert procurement, vendor-management, and opportunity-discovery interest while giving AI7Lab a first-party canonical product narrative.

### Sequence

1. Clear product proposition.
2. Metric proof: 134,000+ vendors and 20+ organisations, with approved definitions.
3. Problems: fragmented vendor information, discovery, qualification, evidence, monitoring, and opportunity matching.
4. Workflow: discover → verify → assess → qualify → monitor or act.
5. Capability groups using only verified product information.
6. Use cases by buyer and industry.
7. Evidence-backed decision principle and human controls.
8. Related procurement research and resources.
9. External product CTA and discussion CTA.

### Suggested H1

> See the vendor. Find the opportunity.

### Supporting copy

> Vendoreye brings supplier discovery, evidence, qualification, monitoring, and opportunity intelligence into one continuously useful vendor record.

## RoleField `/products/rolefield`

### Objective

Convert customer-operations interest by presenting RoleField as the operating layer across support, calling, call-centre workflows, and in-person service through Seer kiosk AI.

### Sequence

1. Whole-operation product proposition.
2. Metric proof: 60M+ calls, about 10M monthly, US$1M+ savings, with approved definitions and clearly framed as evidence from RoleField's calling operations.
3. Four operating surfaces: support, calling, call-centre operations, and Seer kiosk AI.
4. Seer story: approved-person recognition and context, device-trained conversations, returning-visitor continuity, supported document capture, and animated avatar interfaces.
5. Operating loop: engage → route → resolve → improve, with approved actions and human escalation.
6. Arabic, English, and multilingual operating context.
7. Use cases across qualification, scheduling, follow-up, service, dispatch, claims intake, physical reception, and other verified workflows.
8. Integration, observability, and human-handoff principles.
9. Related customer-operations, voice, and kiosk research.
10. External product CTA and customer-operations discussion CTA.

### Suggested H1

> The operating layer for every customer interaction.

### Supporting copy

> RoleField brings customer support, calling and call-centre operations into one AI system, then extends the experience into physical spaces through Seer kiosk AI.

## Enterprise AI `/enterprise-ai`

### Objective

Position AI7Lab as an experienced product builder that can work directly on an organisation’s operational problem without appearing to be a broad traditional consultancy.

### Sequence

1. Outcome-led hero.
2. Problem-selection criteria: value, volume, friction, data, actionability, risk, and ownership.
3. Working model:
   - Frame the problem and baseline.
   - Prove the valuable workflow.
   - Integrate with systems and controls.
   - Operate, measure, and improve.
4. Types of work: agentic workflows, customer operations, supplier intelligence, document/media intelligence, and adjacent validated problems.
5. Production principles: security, observability, model choice, cost, approvals, and human escalation.
6. Evidence from AI7Lab’s own products.
7. Engagement fit and non-fit.
8. Discuss-a-problem form.

### Suggested H1

> Start with the business problem. Build the AI around it.

### Fit criteria

- A named workflow and accountable owner.
- A measurable baseline or desired outcome.
- Access to representative data and users.
- A credible path to integration and operational ownership.

### Non-fit

- AI added only for novelty.
- No process owner or success measure.
- Unbounded experimentation with no route to production.
- Automation of high-impact decisions without suitable human authority.

## Technology `/technology`

### Objective

Explain why AI7Lab systems can move from prototype to durable operation and evolve without rebuilding everything around each new model.

### Sequence

1. “Built to evolve” hero.
2. Context-to-action architecture: inputs → intelligence → tools/actions → controls → evidence.
3. Agentic orchestration and workflow state.
4. Voice, language, document, visual, retrieval, and structured-data capabilities.
5. Integrations and enterprise actions.
6. Evaluation, observability, policy, and human control.
7. Replaceability and stable interfaces.
8. Taed as a concrete tool built from these principles.
9. Relevant technical research.

### Suggested H1

> AI systems designed to evolve.

### Avoid

- A fixed seven-layer diagram that implies every engagement requires the full stack.
- Leading with GPU procurement or deployment topology.
- Suggesting model choice alone creates defensible value.

## Taed showcase `/technology/taed`

### Objective

Showcase Taed as a serious AI7Lab-built tool and connect its document/visual intelligence to the broader product and enterprise story.

### Sequence

1. Taed descriptor and role inside AI7Lab.
2. Problem: unstructured documents and media do not arrive as reliable business records.
3. Input-to-output demonstration:
   - Documents, images, video, or audio.
   - Classification and extraction.
   - Schema-controlled structured output.
   - Validation and human verification.
   - Versioned and monitored API use.
4. Where it fits: vendor onboarding, due diligence, claims, logistics documents, and other source-backed examples.
5. Relationship to Vendoreye and agentic workflows.
6. Production safeguards and testing principles.
7. Published API milestone, once approved.
8. Taed research collection.
9. External Taed link or enterprise discussion CTA.

### Suggested H1

> Turn unstructured media into structured intelligence.
