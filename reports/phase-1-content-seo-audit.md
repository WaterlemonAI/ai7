# AI7Lab website rehaul — Phase 1 content and SEO audit

Audit date: 13 October 2026  
Primary domain: https://www.ai7lab.net  
Repository: `ai-7-lab-website-blueprint`  
Scope: current source, production build, local crawl, and public live-site checks

## Executive summary

AI7Lab has a technically healthy website with a much larger search footprint than the primary navigation suggests. The current sitemap exposes 238 indexable URLs: 13 core pages, 2 career pages, and 223 insight articles. The site also publishes 30 downloadable resources and dedicated discovery files for search engines and AI systems.

The redesign should therefore be treated as an SEO migration, not a visual replacement.

The principal issue is a mismatch between the current site and the new business structure. The live site primarily positions AI7Lab as a sovereign-AI infrastructure and delivery company. It presents Taed, Vendoreye, and RoleField as three equivalent active products. The approved future hierarchy is two principal products—Vendoreye and RoleField—with Taed retained as an additional AI7Lab-built visual/document-intelligence tool and technical showcase.

Core technical SEO is already sound. The migration should preserve the domain, valuable URLs, articles, downloads, metadata framework, structured data, analytics, sitemap, robots policy, and LLM discovery files while rewriting the company narrative and product hierarchy.

## Current positioning audit

### Current public story

The live homepage leads with “Sovereign AI · End to end” and “Build AI you control.” The narrative emphasizes private infrastructure, GPUs, deployment boundaries, model control, governance, and a seven-discipline delivery loop.

The product portfolio is currently described as:

- Taed: visual intelligence APIs.
- Vendoreye: supplier intelligence and operations.
- RoleField: multilingual conversational AI and agentic execution.

The current positioning is repeated in global metadata, the homepage, vision, products, platform, technology, partners, careers, contact, footer, structured data descriptions, social imagery, sitemap content, `llms.txt`, and `llms-full.txt`.

### New business story supplied for the rehaul

The new brief establishes AI7Lab as:

- A builder of products based on agentic AI and current technologies.
- A partner to leading organizations adopting AI to solve business problems.
- Focused on reducing costs, improving efficiency, and pushing what is operationally possible.
- A continuous product-development company that identifies ecosystem problems and creates valuable solutions.
- The owner of two current products: Vendoreye and RoleField.
- The builder of additional AI tools and reusable technical capabilities, including Taed.

### Messaging gap

The existing narrative is infrastructure-first and control-first. The new narrative is product-first, outcome-first, and agentic-AI-first. Sovereignty, governance, infrastructure, and future-proof architecture may remain supporting proof points, but they should no longer dominate the company definition unless the business confirms otherwise.

## Product and claim inventory

### Vendoreye

Current site positioning:

- Supplier discovery, verification, assessment, qualification, governance, and monitoring.
- Evidence-backed vendor records.
- Vendor onboarding and lifecycle management.
- Procurement and third-party-risk guides across six industries.

New claims supplied for the rehaul:

- More than 134,000 vendors.
- More than 20 organizations use or trust Vendoreye for their next opportunity.

Required before publication:

- Define whether 134,000 refers to registered, indexed, verified, active, or addressable vendors.
- Approve the exact meaning of “20+ organizations trust Vendoreye.”
- Add an “as of” date or establish an automated update source.
- Confirm whether customer logos, names, testimonials, or anonymized cases may be shown.

### RoleField

Current site positioning:

- Multilingual voice agents for GCC workflows.
- Arabic and multilingual speech-to-speech capability.
- Qualification, scheduling, follow-up, CRM actions, and human handoffs.
- Six industry-specific guides.

Phase 3 scope correction for the rehaul:

- RoleField is not positioned only as voice AI. It is the broader customer-operations platform spanning support, calling, and call-centre operations.
- Seer extends RoleField into approved physical environments through kiosk AI, with source-backed concepts including enrolled-visitor recognition, contextual profiles, device-trained conversations, returning-visitor continuity, supported business-card and identity-document capture modes, and animated avatar interfaces.
- Existing call and savings metrics remain valuable proof of the calling operation, but must not visually or verbally define the entire product.

New claims supplied for the rehaul:

- More than 60 million calls completed.
- Approximately 10 million calls per month.
- More than US$1 million saved for customers.

Required before publication:

- Define whether “calls” means attempted, connected, handled, or completed calls.
- Confirm the measurement window for 10 million calls per month.
- Define the savings methodology, baseline, currency, and customer scope.
- Add an “as of” date.
- Confirm whether these are cumulative RoleField totals or include predecessor systems.

### Taed — approved role in the new portfolio

Taed will remain part of the AI7Lab story, but not as a third peer to the two principal products. It should be positioned as an additional tool in AI7Lab’s technical belt and as concrete proof of the lab’s ability to turn AI research into production-grade capabilities.

The existing source supports the following showcase narrative:

- Production-grade visual intelligence for documents, images, video, and audio.
- Structured, schema-controlled outputs rather than ungoverned model responses.
- Versioned and monitored APIs suitable for reusable enterprise workflows.
- Document classification, extraction, validation, and human-verification patterns.
- Arabic and English document-information extraction.
- Secure document-intelligence pipelines and production testing.
- A reusable component that can support other AI7Lab products and customer workflows, including vendor onboarding.
- A published milestone of more than 1,000 API calls per day, subject to confirming the date and whether the claim remains current.

Taed currently affects:

- The homepage and product overview.
- Global company and product descriptions.
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`.
- Product imagery and Open Graph content.
- Six dedicated applied-product guides.
- Ten dedicated research URLs containing “taed” in the slug.
- One current career page.
- Multiple announcements and calls to action.
- Numerous general research and “AI in X” articles that reference Taed.

All existing Taed URLs should be retained. Their calls to action and entity language should be updated so they support the “AI7Lab tool and capability showcase” position rather than presenting Taed as a third principal product. Historical announcements should remain dated historical records.

The new design should give Taed a substantial showcase rather than reducing it to a logo card. The showcase should explain the problem, input types, schema-controlled workflow, production safeguards, example enterprise uses, relationship to Vendoreye and other agentic systems, current milestone evidence, and links to the existing Taed research library.

## Technical SEO baseline

### Passing checks

- Production build completes successfully with TypeScript validation.
- All 238 sitemap URLs return HTTP 200 in the local production build.
- No sitemap page is missing a title, meta description, canonical URL, or H1.
- Every sitemap page has exactly one H1.
- Titles, descriptions, and canonical URLs are unique across the sitemap.
- No sitemap page contains `noindex`.
- The internal crawl found 267 unique internal paths and no broken internal links.
- JSON-LD parses successfully on every sitemap page.
- Global Organization and WebSite structured data is present.
- Job pages include JobPosting and BreadcrumbList data.
- Applied product guides include BlogPosting, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList data.
- Research articles include BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList data.
- “AI in X” articles include Article data.
- Canonical hostname redirects work: HTTP and non-`www` requests redirect to `https://www.ai7lab.net/`.
- Vendoreye and RoleField external product sites return HTTP 200.
- The live homepage, sitemap, robots file, and LLM discovery file return HTTP 200.
- Robots rules allow normal search crawlers and named AI crawlers while disallowing `/api/`.
- No rendered image is missing an `alt` attribute. Empty alt text is used extensively for decorative imagery and should be spot-checked during design QA.

### Issues and opportunities

| Finding | Scale | Risk | Recommendation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Search titles longer than 60 characters | 123 of 238 pages | Search result truncation; key terms or brand may be cut | Rewrite by template, prioritizing core and high-traffic pages |
| Meta descriptions longer than 160 characters | 56 pages | Truncated or rewritten snippets | Create tighter template rules, especially for all 50 “AI in X” pages |
| Meta descriptions shorter than 70 characters | 2 pages | Weak search-result proposition | Expand with specific search intent and value |
| Core copy conflicts with new business model | Most primary pages | Confusing brand/entity signals | Rewrite all primary-page metadata and copy in one coordinated migration |
| Taed is declared as an equal third product | Site-wide | Conflicts with the approved two-product hierarchy | Reclassify Taed as an additional AI7Lab-built tool and capability showcase |
| Contact form opens a local mail client | Main conversion path | Fragile conversion experience and limited attribution | Replace or supplement with a measured form in the implementation phase |
| GA measurement exists, but no conversion taxonomy was found in source | Site-wide | Rehaul impact may be hard to measure | Define product interest, enterprise enquiry, resource download, and outbound-product events |
| Sitemap `lastModified` values are manually hard-coded | All sitemap families | Dates can become stale or misleading | Derive dates from content or maintain a deliberate release date source |
| Insight index is very large | `/insights` | Heavy initial document and difficult discovery | Redesign taxonomy, pagination/filtering, and internal-linking without changing article URLs |

The 60/160-character thresholds are prioritization heuristics, not search-engine requirements.

## Search visibility baseline

Public search checks show that the domain and multiple core and research pages are discoverable, including the homepage, products, technology, vision, platform, insights, partners, contact, careers, announcements, and an agent-security research article.

However, public search sampling cannot provide a reliable index count, rankings, clicks, impressions, backlink value, or query-level performance.

No Google Search Console, Google Analytics reporting API, or backlink-platform credentials were available in the repository or connected tools. The source contains Google Analytics property `G-9PWLKTW3XB`, but source access alone does not expose historical reports.

Before redirect or consolidation decisions, export at least the previous 16 months of:

- Search Console pages, queries, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position.
- Google Analytics landing pages, sessions, conversions, outbound product clicks, and resource downloads.
- Backlinks and referring domains by destination URL, if a backlink tool is available.
- Any paid campaign or email links pointing to current pages or downloadable assets.

## Content disposition

### Retain

- Primary domain and canonical hostname.
- `/products`, `/insights`, `/announcements`, `/careers`, and `/contact`.
- All existing insight URLs until performance data says otherwise.
- All downloadable-resource URLs until inbound-link data is reviewed.
- Organization identity, DIFC/Dubai location, contact details, and company LinkedIn.
- Vendoreye and RoleField product associations.
- Existing metadata utilities, schema patterns, Open Graph generation, sitemap, robots policy, analytics, and LLM discovery endpoints.
- High-value themes that support the new story: agent security, evaluation, governance, multilingual AI, voice AI, procurement AI, workflow automation, production readiness, ROI, and proprietary AI IP.

### Rewrite

- Homepage and global company description.
- Vision/company page.
- Product overview and product proof.
- Platform and technology narratives.
- Enterprise, builder, and provider partnership propositions.
- Contact and conversion copy.
- Careers positioning.
- Footer and global calls to action.
- Open Graph text and imagery.
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`.
- Internal links and article calls to action that currently present Taed as an equal principal product or promote sovereign-AI-stack engagements.

### Preserve but de-emphasize

- Evergreen sovereign-AI, infrastructure, model-control, governance, and deployment research that remains factually useful.
- Older announcements that accurately represent history.
- Broad “AI in X” educational content that attracts relevant enterprise visitors but is not the main company message.

Historical content should not be silently rewritten to imply that a past announcement happened under the new positioning.

### Consolidation candidates — do not act before data review

- `/vision` and a future About/company page.
- `/platform` and `/technology`.
- `/partners/enterprises` and a future enterprise-AI page.
- Builder and technology-provider partnership pages if those programs are no longer strategic.
- Duplicate or overlapping research articles after query and backlink analysis.

### Remove or redirect only after confirmation

- The Taed partnership job if the vacancy is closed.
- Any other expired job pages.
- Partnership pages for programs the business no longer offers.

## Phase 1 decisions required

These inputs are required before Phase 2 messaging and information architecture can be finalized:

1. Are the current builder and technology-provider partnership programs still part of the business?
2. Are both current job listings open?
3. What is the primary site conversion: enterprise project enquiry, product discovery, investor/partner enquiry, or a prioritized combination?
4. Which customer names, logos, case studies, or testimonials may be published?
5. What are the approved definitions and “as of” dates for all Vendoreye and RoleField metrics?
6. Is the published Taed milestone of more than 1,000 API calls per day still approved for use, and what is its measurement date?
7. Can Search Console, Analytics, and backlink exports be supplied before URL consolidation?

## Phase 1 conclusion

The site does not need an SEO rebuild from zero. It needs a disciplined narrative and entity migration on top of a healthy technical foundation.

The safest strategy is to retain the current URL estate through the first redesign release, rewrite the primary experience around agentic-AI products and enterprise outcomes, add first-party Vendoreye and RoleField detail pages, give Taed a rich but clearly secondary tool showcase, preserve the insight library, and postpone destructive URL consolidation until first-party traffic and backlink evidence is available.
